Em entrevista em inglês, o problema quase nunca é o que você sabe. É entender a pergunta de primeira, organizar a ideia e achar as palavras, tudo ao mesmo tempo. A boa notícia: **as perguntas se repetem muito**, então dá para chegar com as respostas pensadas.

Aqui estão as 12 perguntas mais comuns, o que o entrevistador quer saber e um exemplo de resposta em inglês para você adaptar.

<!-- cta -->

## Como preparar suas respostas

- **Curto e organizado:** de 30 a 60 segundos por resposta.
- **Exemplos reais:** um projeto, um número, um resultado.
- **Em voz alta:** treine falando, não só lendo. Grave no celular e escute uma vez.

## As 12 perguntas

### 1. "Tell me about yourself."

Querem um resumo profissional, não sua biografia. Use presente, passado e futuro.

> "I'm a frontend developer with three years of experience in React and TypeScript. At my current company, an e-commerce in São Paulo, I rebuilt the checkout page, which increased completed orders by about 15%. Now I'm looking for a remote role at an international company, and this position is a great match."

Para mais exemplos, use nosso [gerador grátis de resposta para "Tell me about yourself"](/tools/tell-me-about-yourself-generator) (em inglês).

### 2. "Why do you want to work here?"

Mostre que você pesquisou a empresa e ligue o que ela faz ao que você quer.

> "I've used your product myself, and I like how simple it is. I want to work on a product people use every day, and my experience with large React apps fits what your team is building."

### 3. "What are your strengths?"

> "I learn fast. When my team moved to TypeScript, I studied it on my own and helped migrate half of our components in two months."

### 4. "What is your greatest weakness?"

Uma fraqueza real, mas não grave, e o que você faz para melhorar.

> "I used to avoid asking for help. Now, if I'm stuck for more than an hour, I ask a teammate, and it saves a lot of time."

### 5. "Tell me about a challenge you faced at work."

Use o método STAR: situação, tarefa, ação e resultado.

> "Our website was very slow on mobile (situation). I was asked to fix it before Black Friday (task). I compressed images and split our JavaScript into smaller files (action). The page loaded twice as fast and we had our best sales day (result)."

### 6. "Why are you leaving your current job?"

> "I've learned a lot there, but I want to work with an international team and on bigger products."

### 7. "Where do you see yourself in five years?"

> "I'd like to be a senior developer, leading projects and helping newer developers grow."

### 8. "How do you deal with tight deadlines?"

> "I break the work into small parts, agree on priorities with my manager, and give updates early if something is at risk."

### 9. "Tell me about a project you're proud of."

Escolha um com resultado mensurável e explique exatamente o seu papel.

### 10. "What are your salary expectations?"

Pesquise antes e dê uma faixa: *"Based on my research, I'm looking for something between X and Y, depending on the full package."*

### 11. "Have you worked remotely before?"

> "Yes, for two years, with teammates in different time zones. I communicate a lot in writing and keep my tasks updated."

### 12. "Do you have any questions for us?"

Sempre diga que sim. Duas boas opções: *"What does a typical week look like in this role?"* e *"What would success look like in the first three months?"*

## Frases úteis para ganhar tempo

- *"That's a good question. Let me think for a second."*
- *"Sorry, could you repeat the question?"*
- *"Just to make sure I understand, are you asking about...?"*
- *"Let me give you an example."*
