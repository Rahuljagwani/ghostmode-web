**How much does an AI interview copilot cost?** More than most people expect. The popular tools charge **$66 to $299 a month**, and some keep the features you need in a live interview, such as staying hidden from screen share, for their most expensive plan.

This guide compares the prices of the main tools as listed on their own websites in **November 2026**, then works out what a month, and what a few real interviews, cost on each. Prices change often and can depend on your country and current promotions, so check each site before you buy.

## What a few interviews really cost

Most people don't interview every day. They have a handful of interviews over a few weeks, then nothing for months. So the useful question is: **what does it cost to cover the interviews I actually have?**

Take **4 one-hour interviews in one month**:

![What 4 interviews cost: Ghost $19 once, ParakeetAI $64, LockedIn AI about $66, Cluely $149.99, Final Round AI $150](/blog-images/ai-interview-copilot-pricing/four-interviews.png "Subscriptions cost the same whether you have 1 interview or 20")

- **Ghost:** a typical interview uses 50 to 60 credits (1 credit per text question, 2 per screenshot, 3 per voice question). The $19 pack has 450 credits, enough for about 7 interviews, so 4 interviews cost **$19 once**, with credits left for later.
- **ParakeetAI:** 4 credits. The cheapest mix is 3 for $39 plus 1 for $25: **$64**.
- **LockedIn AI:** one month of the Credits plan, **about $66** with the current promo.
- **Cluely:** **$149.99** for the plan that stays hidden while you share your screen.
- **Final Round AI:** **$150** for a single month.

## Things to check besides the price

1. **Does the free plan include live help?** Several free plans don't, so you can't test the tool in a real interview before paying.
2. **Is screen-share hiding on every plan?** On some tools it's only on the top plan. If you share your screen in Zoom, Google Meet or Teams, check this first.
3. **Does it renew automatically?** Monthly and yearly plans keep charging until you cancel. Credit packs don't.
4. **Do credits expire?** Ghost's and ParakeetAI's credits don't, so they carry over to your next job search.
5. **What's the refund policy?** Many tools, including Ghost, don't refund once you've bought, so use the free plan to check it works with your setup.
6. **Which platforms?** Some tools are browser-based, others are desktop apps for macOS and Windows.

## Which pricing model suits you?

- **A few interviews over a few weeks:** one-time credits. You pay only for what you use, and there's nothing to cancel.
- **Interviewing every day for months:** an unlimited plan can work out cheaper per interview, if you're sure you'll use it.
- **Want to try before paying:** pick a tool whose free plan includes live help.
