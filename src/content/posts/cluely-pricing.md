**Cluely** is one of the best-known AI meeting and interview assistants, and **"how much does Cluely cost?"** is one of the first questions people ask about it. The short answer: the main paid plan is **$19.99 a month**, but the plan that stays hidden while you share your screen is **$149.99 a month**.

Here's every plan, what it includes, and what it means if you want to use Cluely in interviews. Prices are from [cluely.com/pricing](https://cluely.com/pricing), checked on **November 16, 2026**. Prices change, so check the page before you buy.

## Cluely's plans

![Cluely's plans: Starter free, Pro $19.99 a month, Pro + Undetectability $149.99 a month, with screen-share hiding only on the top plan](/blog-images/cluely-pricing/plans.png "Staying hidden on screen share is only on the most expensive plan")

| Plan | Price | What's included | Hidden from screen share? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Starter** | Free | Limited AI responses, limited meeting notetaking, custom instructions, up to 3 files, ask AI about past meetings | No |
| **Pro** | $19.99 a month | Everything in Starter, plus unlimited AI responses, unlimited notetaking, the latest AI models, priority support, unlimited files | No |
| **Pro + Undetectability** | $149.99 a month | Everything in Pro, plus "completely hidden to meeting screen sharing software" | Yes |

The pricing page has a monthly and annual switch, and its title mentions Pro **"from $11.99/mo"**, which usually means the annual price per month. The page didn't list annual prices for each plan when we checked.

## What Cluely costs for interviews

Cluely is designed as an everyday meeting assistant: notes, answers and follow-ups across all your calls. For **interviews**, one feature matters most: if you share your screen in Zoom, Google Meet or Microsoft Teams, you need the assistant **not to appear** in what the interviewer sees.

On Cluely, that's only on **Pro + Undetectability at $149.99 a month**. So:

- **1 interview this month:** $149.99.
- **4 interviews this month:** $149.99.
- **2 interviews a month for three months:** about $450, unless you cancel and resubscribe each time.

If you don't share your screen, **Pro at $19.99 a month** may be enough.

## When Cluely's pricing makes sense

- You want an **always-on assistant for every meeting**, every day, and will use the unlimited responses and notetaking.
- You're in a long, busy interview season and will use it often enough to justify a monthly plan.
- You don't need to stay hidden on screen share, so Pro covers you.

## A cheaper option for interviews

If you mainly need help in a **handful of interviews**, a monthly plan means paying for weeks you don't use. **Ghost by Renekin AI** is an interview copilot that charges once for credits instead:

- **Hidden from screen share on every plan**, including the free one, on macOS and Windows.
- **20 free credits** with every feature unlocked, so you can test it in a real interview first. No card needed.
- **One-time packs:** $10 (200 credits, about 3 to 4 interviews), $19 (450 credits, about 7) and $28 (750 credits, about 12 to 15). Credits never expire, and nothing renews.
- A typical interview uses 50 to 60 credits, about **$2 to $3**.
